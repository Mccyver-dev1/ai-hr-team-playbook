# AI HR Service Model

## Objective
Provide end-to-end HR AI services by allowing a client to select specialist agents according to business pain points.

## Core principle
We do not sell a fixed number of AI employees. The catalog is a starting set of specialist capabilities. A client may select one agent, a workflow bundle, or a broader HR operating model.

## Service flow
1. Identify business pain
2. Map pain to HR work
3. Select AI specialists
4. Configure data, rules, prompts and approval points
5. Pilot the workflow
6. Measure outcomes
7. Expand or add specialists

## Human authority
AI may analyze, draft, classify, summarize, validate and recommend. High-impact employment decisions remain with authorized humans.

## Market benchmark
Adecco Thailand Salary Guide 2026 is used only as a market reference for comparable human HR roles and salary ranges. It is not the product, not a sponsor, and not the price of the AI service.
