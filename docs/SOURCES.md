## Important
The project maps AI roles to human-equivalent HR roles. Because one AI role can span multiple real jobs, the displayed cost model is deliberately illustrative and should be replaced with the organization's actual headcount plan, location, seniority, benefits, statutory cost, and vendor quotations before budgeting.
